Privacy Policy – Voxel1_Daily

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Developer: Voxel1

Contact: kesari.payneni@voxel1.com

1. Introduction

Voxel1_Daily is an employee attendance app used by Voxel1 staff to record check-in and check-out, apply for leave and manage daily work tasks. The app is available on Android and on the web at www.voxel1.co.in. It is for Voxel1 employees only; accounts are created by Voxel1 administrators, not by the public.

We respect the privacy of our users and handle their personal information responsibly. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

  • Employee details: name, employee ID, mobile number, work email, department, role and, if your administrator adds one, a profile photo.
  • Login information: your mobile number and the credentials and session tokens used to sign you in securely.
  • Attendance records: check-in and check-out dates and times, attendance status, work schedules and office.
  • Location: your device's precise (GPS) location, collected only at the moment you check in or check out, to confirm you are at one of the Voxel1 offices. The coordinates are saved with that attendance record. Location is not tracked in the background or at any other time.
  • Leave and task information: leave requests, approvals, holidays, tasks, task notes and announcements you create or receive.
  • Device and technical information: basic device, browser and app information and error logs needed for security and to keep the app working. If you allow notifications, a push-notification token for your device.

We do not collect contacts, messages, call logs, files or camera images from your device, and we do not use the camera or microphone.

3. How we use information

  • Record and manage employee attendance, including verifying that check-ins happen at an office.
  • Process leave requests and assign and track work tasks.
  • Let authorised Voxel1 managers and administrators review attendance, leave and task records.
  • Generate attendance and monthly reports.
  • Send in-app and push notifications about leave, tasks and announcements.
  • Keep the app secure, reliable and working, and provide technical support.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with advertisers.

4. Data sharing

Your attendance, leave and task information is visible to authorised managers and administrators at Voxel1.

We use service providers that process data on our behalf only to run the app: Supabase (database, authentication and file storage) and Vercel (web hosting). Push notifications are delivered through your device or browser's notification service (for example Google Firebase Cloud Messaging).

We disclose personal information otherwise only where required by law.

5. Data security

All data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Access to records is restricted by role, so employees see their own records and only authorised managers and administrators can see team records. We use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure and loss.

6. Data retention and deletion

Attendance and employee information is kept for as long as you are employed at Voxel1 and afterwards for as long as required by Voxel1's record-keeping needs and applicable law. It is then deleted.

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, including deletion of your account, by emailing kesari.payneni@voxel1.com. We will respond within 30 days and handle the request in line with applicable legal and employment requirements.

7. Device permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate): used only when you tap check-in or check-out, to confirm you are at a Voxel1 office. You can deny it, but office check-in will not work without it.
  • Notifications (optional): used to alert you about leave decisions, tasks and announcements.
  • Internet: required to load the app and save your records.

8. Children

Voxel1_Daily is intended for Voxel1 employees only and is not directed at children under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features, data practices or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
